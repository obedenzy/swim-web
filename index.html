Frequently Asked Questions

What age do you start lessons?

We offer classes for all ages, from toddlers (starting at 6 months with a parent) to adults. Our curriculum is tailored to each age group and skill level.

Are your instructors certified?

Yes, all of our swimming instructors are certified and have extensive experience teaching a wide range of students.

What is the cost of a class?

Our pricing varies based on the course and number of sessions. Please see our pricing section above for detailed information on our Basic, Standard, and Premium plans.

What should I bring to my first class?

For your first class, please bring a swimsuit, towel, and a pair of goggles. Swim caps are optional but recommended.
